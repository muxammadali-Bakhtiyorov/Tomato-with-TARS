# Legumes, Cover Crops & Specialty Rotations

## Chickpeas (Garbanzo)
- **Nitrogen Fixation**: Partners with *Mesorhizobium* bacteria to fix **45–80 kg N/ha** while using less than half the water of corn or tomatoes.
- **Drainage Rule**: Deep taproot hates "wet feet"—root rot (*Ascochyta* / *Fusarium*) strikes rapidly in saturated heavy clay or standing spring water. Best on well-drained slopes or raised beds.
- **Harvest & Soil Legacy**: Leave root crowns intact in the ground at harvest so decomposing nodules feed the next heavy feeder (tomatoes, peppers, or corn).

## Crimson Clover (Nitrogen Cover Crop)
- **Soil Fertility Engine**: Fixes **65–110 kg N/ha** and adds dense fine-root organic matter that improves soil crumb structure and water holding capacity.
- **Termination Timing**: Mow, crimp, or lightly turn in at **50–75% bloom** (2–3 weeks before transplanting tomatoes or melons) for peak N release without tying up soil moisture.

## White Mustard (Biofumigant Brassica)
- **Pest & Nematode Reset**: Releases natural glucosinolate compounds (isothiocyanates) when chopped green and lightly tilled/irrigated into warm moist soil, suppressing root-knot nematodes and *Verticillium* wilt before nightshades return.
- **Fast Biomass**: Establishes rapidly in cool shoulder seasons (spring or autumn), scavenging leftover nutrients and smothering early broadleaf weeds.

## Melons (Watermelon & Cantaloupe)
- **Pollination & Heat**: Loves warm days (**24–34°C**), sandy-loam warmth, and bee activity in morning hours (avoid spraying insecticides during bloom).
- **Sweetness (Brix) Rule**: Needs steady moisture during vine run and fruit sizing, then **cut irrigation back slightly 7–10 days before harvest**—excess late water splits rinds and dilutes sugars.

## Onions (Allium)
- **Root Depth & Water**: Very shallow, unbranched roots (top **15–25 cm** only)—depends on top-layer moisture (SMAP surface moisture) more than deep root moisture. Needs frequent light drip early, then full dry-down once necks fall over for long storage curing.
- **Disease Break**: Excellent non-host break crop against nightshade wilts and brassica clubroot.

## Managed Fallow / Mulched Rest
- **Dryland Moisture Banking**: Only works if covered with straw stubble or dead mulch. Bare, unmulched fallow loses **40–60% of stored water** to evaporation and wind crusting while starving beneficial soil mycorrhizae.

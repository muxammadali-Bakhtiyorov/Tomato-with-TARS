# Nightshades (Solanaceae): Tomatoes, Peppers & Potatoes

## Shared Disease & Rotation Rule
- Tomatoes, sweet/chili peppers, potatoes, and eggplant share soil-borne pathogens: Verticillium wilt, Fusarium wilt, root-knot nematodes (*Meloidogyne*), and *Alternaria* early blight.
- Minimum break: **2–3 non-nightshade seasons** before returning any nightshade to the same plot. Ideal follow-up crops: biofumigant white mustard, cereal wheat/rye, crimson clover, or onions.

## Tomatoes
- **Ideal Soil & pH**: Deep well-drained loam, pH **6.0–6.8** (still productive up to 7.4 if organic matter >2.5%).
- **Thermal Thresholds**: Frost kills foliage below **2–4°C**. Optimal fruit set occurs at **18–29°C**; pollen turns sterile above **32–34°C** daytime or **22°C** nighttime, causing blossom drop even when watered well.
- **Moisture & Blossom-End Rot**: Keep root-zone moisture steady (**42–60%**). Sharp dry-to-wet swings block calcium transport to developing fruit and trigger blossom-end rot (leathery black bottoms)—irrigation consistency matters more than spraying extra calcium.
- **Canopy & Disease Prevention**: Keep leaves dry by switching from overhead sprinklers to early-morning drip + **5–7 cm straw mulch**. Prune lower suckers touching soil once plants pass 45 cm.

## Peppers (Bell & Chili)
- **Thermal & Water Sensitivity**: Slower early vigor than tomatoes; transplant only after night lows stay consistently **>= 12°C**. Sunscald damages exposed fruit skin above **35°C**—maintain healthy leaf canopy or 30% shade cloth in arid peaks.
- **Irrigation**: Shallow fibrous roots need lighter, more frequent drip cycles than tomatoes; waterlogged clay causes *Phytophthora* crown rot quickly.

## Potatoes
- **Soil Structure & Tubers**: Loose, aerated sandy-loam or silt-loam prevents misshapen tubers and scab. Acidic-to-neutral pH **5.3–6.5** suppresses common scab (*Streptomyces scabies*); above pH 7.2, scab risk climbs sharply—avoid fresh lime or high-salt manure right before planting.
- **Moisture Timing**: Critical water demand during **tuber initiation and bulking** (flowering window). Uneven water causes hollow heart and growth cracks. Stop irrigation 10–14 days before harvest to set skins.
- **Hilling**: Hill soil 15–20 cm around stems to block sunlight (prevents toxic green solanine skin) and cool shallow tubers.
